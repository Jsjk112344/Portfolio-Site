Collabs · Our belief

Small businesses have better chances of doing well if we work together instead of compete

Whether it’s hosting pop-ups at our Seletar Hills outlet or organising co-branded events together, at Prox Coffee we truly believe in the power of collaboration.

There’s always a win-win to be found in every partnership, and we are hardwired to keep looking for such opportunities. The biggest winner? Our customers!

There is no one fixed way of how we could work together — indeed the fun is in brainstorming and designing entirely new ways for our mutual customers to enjoy what we offer.
